Proof of Profit (PoP) is a protocol designed to bring verifiable transparency to real-world business economics using blockchain technology.

The long-term vision of PoP is to create a universal standard for representing real economic activity on-chain — not as promises, forecasts, or financial instruments, but as verifiable facts.

PoP aims to bridge the gap between off-chain businesses and decentralized governance by providing:
- standardized economic data models
- verifiable reporting mechanisms
- DAO-based decision-making over real data

PoP does not aim to replace accounting systems, auditors, or regulators. Instead, it introduces an additional transparency layer that allows communities, DAOs, and protocols to reason about real economic activity without relying on trust.

In the long term, PoP can be applied to:
- food businesses
- manufacturing
- energy
- services
- agriculture
- local and global cooperatives

Pizza is only the first demonstrative case.
