
Most real-world businesses operate behind opaque economic walls.

Even when businesses publish reports, these reports are:
- difficult to verify
- incompatible across businesses and difficult to compare or aggregate
- detached from governance mechanisms
- inaccessible to communities and DAOs

In Web3, this creates a major disconnect:
- DAOs cannot reason about real-world performance
- Communities cannot verify economic claims
- Tokens are often forced into speculative or financial narratives

There is no open standard for:
- representing real business costs
- verifying off-chain operational data
- connecting that data to decentralized governance

Proof of Profit addresses this gap by introducing a protocol-level approach to economic transparency.
