# Off-chain Data Model

PoP introduces a standardized model for representing business economics.

## Core Categories

### Revenue
- product sales
- service income
- refunds (negative revenue)

### Variable Costs
- raw materials
- ingredients
- packaging

### Fixed Costs
- rent
- utilities
- internet
- software

### Labor
- number of workers
- total payroll
- contractor payments

### Taxes
- applicable local taxes
- VAT / sales tax
- social contributions

## Design Principles
- human-readable
- comparable across businesses
- jurisdiction-agnostic where possible
