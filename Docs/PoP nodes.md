# PoP Nodes

A PoP Node is a real-world business that participates in the Proof of Profit protocol.

A PoP Node:
- submits standardized economic data
- follows PoP reporting rules
- exposes its operational transparency voluntarily

PoP Nodes are not required to:
- issue tokens
- share profits
- guarantee performance

They simply provide verifiable economic data to the protocol.

PoP Nodes may be:
- independent businesses
- DAO-owned entities
- community-operated ventures
