# Funding Philosophy

PoP treats funding as fuel for protocol development, not as an investment product.

Funding mechanisms may include:
- grants
- community contributions
- token distribution for governance

Funding is used to support:
- protocol research and specification
- reference implementations and tooling
- simulations and educational models
- experimental real-world pilot projects, such as a demonstration pizzeria
- audits, documentation, and community infrastructure

PoP explicitly avoids:
- profit guarantees
- yield promises
- financial marketing narratives

The protocol prioritizes sustainability, transparency, and long-term alignment over short-term fundraising.
